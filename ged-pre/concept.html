Science Syllabus Index

Complete GED Science curriculum spanning 15 structured chapters.

New to science? You can start here.

What does GED Science test? It checks how well you read scientific information, understand experiments, interpret tables and graphs, and use evidence to choose a reasonable conclusion. You do not need prior science knowledge to begin: the test provides information you can use.

What will you learn? You will practice asking testable questions, understanding life, physical, and Earth science ideas, reading data, and using basic math in scientific situations. The course starts with reading and investigations, moves to tables and graphs, then uses science math to interpret measurements before building into life, matter, motion, energy, and Earth science. Reading, graphs, and experiments work together: read the question, inspect the evidence, then explain what the evidence supports.

Try this real-life example. A student gives similar plants different amounts of water and measures their height after two weeks:

Plant height after two weeks
Water each day (mL)Plant height (cm)

7513

Read the column labels and compare the numbers: in this small example, plants given more water grew taller, though growth increased less between 50 and 75 mL. If the student kept light, soil, and plant type the same, the results support a link between water and growth. The table alone does not prove that water was the only cause.
